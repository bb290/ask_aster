Situation: an Owner asks Sagareus to list their property on Facebook Marketplace, or asks why it is not listed there.

Effective March 2026, Sagareus does not advertise rental listings on Facebook Marketplace as part of its standard marketing strategy. This policy was implemented after a 12-month testing period.

## Key Points

- **Policy violations** — listings were flagged for commercial activity. The platform is not designed for professional property managers.
- **Account limitations** — Facebook Marketplace requires personal accounts. Sagareus does not require employees to use personal social media for business operations.
- **Low applicant quality** — the lowest conversion rate from inquiry to approved Resident of any platform tested.
- **Prospect drop-off** — many inquiries stopped once prospects learned the property is professionally managed and requires a formal application.

## Owner Option

Owners may post on Facebook Marketplace independently and forward inquiries to their Leasing Agent. Based on Sagareus testing, response rates after management contact are minimal.

## Owner Inquiry

Reply to the original email thread.

> Thanks for the suggestion! Sagareus tested Facebook Marketplace for over a year. We found it produced very low-quality leads and a high drop-off rate once prospects learned the property is professionally managed. Because Facebook Marketplace also requires posting from personal accounts, we no longer use it for listings, though you're welcome to post the property yourself and forward any inquiries if you'd like.
