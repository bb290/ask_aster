Non-negotiable rules governing how Sagareus conducts leasing activity. Each policy has its own document; this page is the index.

## Policies

- A Sagareus Leasing Staff member must accompany every showing. Unattended showings are never permitted.
- Properties may be listed no more than 4 weeks prior to Move Out.
- Occupied showings require both a well-maintained property and a cooperative Resident.
- Facebook Marketplace is not part of Sagareus standard marketing strategy.
- Sagareus does not install "For Rent" or "For Lease" signs at managed properties.
