Pre-leasing starts the listing before the unit is vacant so it leases quickly once the current Resident moves out.

Properties may be listed 2 to 4 weeks prior to Move Out, and no more than 4 weeks early.

## Requirements

- **Listing photos must be available** — do not list without photos. See [Rental Listing Standards](rental-listing-standards.md).
- **Use the availability date feature in Buildium** — set availability to the expected move-in date. Never set it to today, and never show the unit as available before it is.
- **Engage early applicants with the Pre-Leasing Form** — applicants who miss out on a property complete the PreLeasing Form.
