Occupied showings reduce vacancy days by starting the leasing process before the current Resident moves out. They are permitted only under specific conditions.

Occupied showings are allowed only when BOTH conditions are met: the property is well-maintained, and the current Resident is cooperative. If either condition fails, use the [Lease Up // Sandbag Approach](lease-up-sandbag-approach.md).

## Before Proposing a Showing

1. Check the most recent Annual Inspection Report.
2. Review completed maintenance tasks. These often include interior photos that show current condition.
3. Confirm the unit still meets the well-maintained condition before contacting the Resident.

## If the Resident Declines

1. Respond politely and respect their decision. A Resident is never required to agree to showings.
2. After a few days, follow up and ask for a walk-through video instead:

> Hi [Resident Name], would you feel comfortable with just me stopping by to capture a quick walk-through video? I've had a lot of interest and am scheduling showings for after move-out, but a video would really help me respond to people in the meantime.

3. Handle the response:
   - **IF yes** — schedule a time. Capture a clean, respectful walk-through video to share with prospects.
   - **IF no** — acknowledge politely and let them know showings will begin after move-out. Use the moment to coordinate move-out tasks if relevant, then move to the [Lease Up // Sandbag Approach](lease-up-sandbag-approach.md).
