Situation: an Owner asks Sagareus to place a "For Rent" or "For Lease" sign at their property, or asks why there isn't one.

Sagareus does not install "For Rent" or "For Lease" signs at managed properties. Do not order, install, or approve signage. This is a standing policy, not a per-property decision. Sagareus used signs in the past and stopped; the security risk is not worth the limited return.

## Key Points

- **A sign advertises a vacancy to everyone who passes, not just renters** — a signed empty house invites trespassing, squatting, and theft of appliances and fixtures. Sagareus has handled repeated break-ins, property damage, and after-hours drama at signed vacancies.
- **The Owner absorbs the downside** — repair costs, insurance claims, and added vacancy days from a break-in land on the Owner. The sign carries none of that risk for anyone else.
- **Sign leads almost never convert** — drive-by inquiries rarely reach a completed application and almost never reach an approved Resident. They consume Leasing Agent time without producing placements.
- **Sign leads distort reporting** — they inflate inquiry counts on the Weekly Activity Report, which makes the funnel look healthier than it is and makes pricing and strategy decisions harder.
- **Fifteen years, one success** — across Brittany's career, a "For Rent" sign produced the desired result exactly once. Break-ins and property damage at signed properties happened many times over.
- **Digital marketing outperforms signage on every measure** — Buildium, Zillow, and the syndication network reach a far larger and better-qualified audience with no security exposure.

## Procedure

1. Do not order, install, or approve signage for any managed property. There is no per-owner exception.
2. If an Owner asks for a sign, send the Owner response below and note the conversation on the LU task.
3. If a sign is already present at listing or onboarding, flag it to the Leasing Manager during the property walk.
4. If an Owner installs a sign themselves, do not remove it. Notify the Leasing Manager, and confirm in writing that the Owner is responsible for the sign and for forwarding any inquiries to the Leasing Agent.

## Owner Inquiry

Reply to the original email thread.

> Thanks for asking about a sign. Sagareus does not install "For Rent" or "For Lease" signs, and I want to explain why, because it's a deliberate policy rather than an oversight.
>
> A yard sign tells everyone who drives past that the home is empty. We've had signed vacancies draw trespassing, squatting, and theft of appliances and fixtures, and the repair costs and added vacancy days land on the owner. After weighing that against what signs actually produce, we stopped using them.
>
> On the return side, signs generate very few inquiries that turn into approved Residents. Across our portfolio, signs have produced almost no qualified applicants, while signed vacancies have repeatedly attracted break-ins and property damage.
>
> The overwhelming majority of qualified applicants find us through Zillow and the platforms we syndicate to, which reach a much larger audience and let us respond and screen quickly. Your property is live on those platforms now, and I'm happy to walk you through the current inquiry and showing activity any time.
