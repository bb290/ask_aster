A member of Sagareus Leasing Staff must accompany every property showing.

Prospective Residents and third parties are never permitted to tour a property unattended. There is no self-showing, lockbox-code, or owner-led exception.

## Key Points

- **Property security** — an unaccompanied visitor has unsupervised access to a vacant home, its appliances, and its fixtures.
- **Consistent information** — every prospect hears the same details about the property, pricing, and applicant criteria from a trained staff member.
- **Professional experience** — the showing is the prospect's first impression of Sagareus and of the Owner's property.
- **Fair housing** — accompanying every showing means every prospect receives the same tour, the same information, and the same opportunity.
