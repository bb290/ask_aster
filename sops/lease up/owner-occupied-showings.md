Applies when the property Owner is living in the home while it is being marketed for lease.

This is not the same as [Lease Up // Occupied Showings](lease-up-occupied-showings.md), which covers a unit occupied by a Resident under an existing lease. Here there is no Resident and no RCW 59.18 notice obligation. Access depends entirely on Owner cooperation.

## Pre-Listing Setup

Complete all three items during a single site visit.

1. **Install a keybox** — the Owner leaves one key inside for agent access. Confirm the key opens every door agents will need. Do not assume it works.
2. **Schedule professional photos and a video walkthrough** — see [Rental Listing Standards](rental-listing-standards.md) for photo requirements. Tell the Owner the home must be clean and decluttered before the shoot: items put away in cupboards, bathrooms and bedrooms organized, common spaces clear, and minimal items left out.
3. **Conduct a turnover inspection** — note anything that must be addressed immediately, separate from cosmetic items that can wait until move-out.

## Showing Schedule

Fair Housing: the Leasing Agent handles all prospect questions during the showing. If the Owner is home, keep conversation between the Owner and prospects to a minimum. An offhand comment from the Owner about a prospect's family, background, or circumstances creates liability for Sagareus even when it is unintentional.

1. Work with the Owner to set 2 to 3 recurring showing windows per week, for example Mondays at 5pm and Wednesdays at 3pm.
2. Group prospects into those windows rather than scheduling one-offs. This limits how often the Owner has to tidy the home and reduces disruption for them.
3. Contact the Owner roughly 3 hours before each showing block to confirm the home will be presentable.

## After Lease Signing

1. Conduct a second turnover inspection and walkthrough with the Owner.
2. Confirm move-out and move-in dates to the Owner in writing.
3. Set the Owner's vacate date approximately 5 days before move-in when turnover work already happened while the Owner lived there and the unit is otherwise rent-ready. This covers final cleaning and any turnover items identified at the second inspection.
