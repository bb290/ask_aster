:::info
* [Renewal & Rent Increase Coordinator](../../docs/role-profiles/renewal-rent-increase-coordinator.md) determines which notice variant applies and emails the owner using the templates below.
* **cc listings@sagareus.com** on every notice. The Owner replies all to confirm re-lease plans or ask questions, which keeps the leasing team in the thread from the start.
* Screenshot the sent email and attach it to this subtask.

:::

## Email Template — Notice to Vacate - End of Lease / Proper Notice Provided

> **Subject: Notice to Vacate - Proper Notice // \[Property Address\]**
>
> \[Owner Name\],
>
> Residents have submitted notice to vacate with a scheduled move-out date of \[Move Out Date\]. Unless we hear otherwise, Sagareus will proceed with sourcing a new Resident for the property. Our leasing team at listings@sagareus.com is copied here, so **reply all** to confirm you would like to re-lease, or to tell us if you have other plans for the property.
>
> There is nothing else you need to do right now. Sagareus will coordinate the Resident's move-out, complete the move-out inspection, and handle deposit withholding.
>
> **What happens next:** our leasing team takes it from here. They will send you the full leasing plan, including recommended pricing, lease terms, and turn over recommendations, before the property is listed.
>
> Please reply all with any questions about your upcoming listing so the leasing team sees them.
>
> Thank you,

>
> State law requires landlords to allow early termination of the lease without penalty in these situations, and the security deposit must be refunded in full.
