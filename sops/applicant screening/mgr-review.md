## Applicant Screening // Leasing Team Lead


1. Receive "DONE" application task in Asana
   * Once Leasing Assistants have gathered all necessary information, they will assign to Leasing Team Lead for approval / decline / negotiation


---

### Leasing Team Lead Procedures


1. Initial Scan → Review all documents
   * Look for inconsistencies or potential fraud; Anything that seems off
   * Proof of Income
     * Verify dates represent the most recent pay period
     * Critically examine → Do these documents seem suspicious at all?
   * Credit Screening
     * Confirm Equifax FICO Score (300-850)
       * This is the only score that qualifies an applicant
       * Disregard the AI Score (RealPage AI Score or similar), even when it appears on the report
