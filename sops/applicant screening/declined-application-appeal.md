Situation: a declined applicant contacts Sagareus to appeal the decision, asks for a second look, or wants to explain the circumstances behind something on their screening report.

## Key Points

- **The appeal goes to the Leasing Manager, not the Leasing Assistant.** Do not debate, re-argue, or reverse a decline. Collect the reason in writing, route the task, and let the Leasing Manager decide.
- **Offer the appeal path to every declined applicant who asks.** Same process, same link, same wording, every time. Offering a second look to some applicants and not others is a fair housing violation.
- **An appeal does not hold the property.** The unit stays available and can be leased to another applicant while an appeal is open.
- **The decline reasons are already documented.** They are listed on the Adverse Action Notice sent under [Declined | Adverse Action](declined-adverse-action.md). Do not restate or expand on them by phone or email.

If the applicant ties the appeal to a disability, whether they are asking for an exception to the criteria or explaining that a disability caused the flag, stop and treat it as a reasonable accommodation request, not a discretionary appeal. Accommodation requests carry their own legal obligations and cannot be declined on the same footing as an ordinary appeal. Flag it explicitly to the Leasing Manager when re-assigning the task.

## Procedure

1. Acknowledge the request. Do not discuss the decision itself.
2. Send the Appeal Request email below from leasing@sagareus.com.
3. Reopen the applicant's Asana application task if it was already marked complete.
4. Post the applicant's reason for appealing as a comment on the task, in their own words.
   - **IF the applicant has not put it in writing yet** — leave the task open and wait for their reply before reassigning.
   - **IF the appeal cites a disability** — note "Possible reasonable accommodation request" at the top of the comment.
   - **IF the applicant is disputing report accuracy** — note it, and confirm they were directed to the consumer reporting agency.
5. Reassign the task to B French (bb@sagareus.com), due today.
   - Brittany French appears in Asana as "B French". Do not assign to Brittany Hubbell (brittany@sagareus.com), who is the Leasing Lead.

## Email Template — Appeal Request

Send from leasing@sagareus.com. Reply to the original email thread.

> Hi [Applicant Name],
>
> Thank you for reaching out. I understand you would like to appeal the decision on your application.
>
> Sagareus reviews every application against the same written criteria, and the specific reasons for the decision are listed on the Adverse Action Notice you received.
>
> If you believe any information on your screening report is inaccurate or incomplete, the fastest path is to contact the consumer reporting agency listed on that notice. They are required to investigate, and you are entitled to a free copy of your report within 60 days.
>
> If there is context you would like our Leasing Manager to consider, here is how to move forward:
>
> 1. Reply to this email with the reason you are appealing, so we have it in writing.
> 2. Schedule a call with our Leasing Manager, Brittany French. Here is a link to schedule a convenient time: https://meetings-na2.hubspot.com/bb19
>
> Please note that submitting an appeal does not place a hold on the property. Unit remains available to other applicants while an appeal is under review.
>
> Regards,

### Example Applicant Questions

> "Can I talk to someone about this? There's an explanation for the collections account."

> "I want to appeal. Who do I speak to?"

> "That eviction on my report isn't mine."

> "Is there anything I can do, like paying a larger deposit or adding a co-signer?"
